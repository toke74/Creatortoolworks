# Business Blueprint

## Business model
A portfolio of free browser-based YouTube creator utilities funded initially by display advertising, with optional later premium/affiliate/API revenue.

## North-star outcome
A creator arrives with a concrete task, completes it quickly, understands the output, and chooses another relevant tool because it is useful—not because navigation is manipulative.

## Revenue stages

### Stage A — Pre-monetization
Optimize product quality, search eligibility, performance, and usage measurement. Keep infrastructure inexpensive.

### Stage B — AdSense
Introduce central, policy-reviewed ad placements. Measure revenue alongside user experience and performance.

### Stage C — Revenue diversification
Test only after sufficient usage evidence: premium exports/batch workflows, ad-free membership, affiliates, sponsorships, saved workspaces, API.

## Cost philosophy
- Prefer deterministic browser logic.
- Avoid paid API calls for commodity calculations.
- Keep persistent infrastructure at zero until needed.
- Treat AI coding subscriptions/API usage as development cost.
- Recheck third-party prices before purchase; do not depend on stale plan prices.

## Growth gates

### Gate: 6 → 20 tools
Proceed when the shared framework is stable, analytics works, and at least several tools show real usage or search demand.

### Gate: 20 → 50 tools
Proceed when category architecture and internal links remain coherent and maintenance does not require per-page custom fixes.

### Gate: 50 → 100 tools
Proceed when source freshness, automated QA, and content review are systematic.

### Gate: 100 → 300 tools
Proceed only if incremental tools continue to bring distinct user value and the site does not become a collection of near-duplicate keyword pages.

## Business metrics
- Monthly organic clicks.
- Monthly active tool users.
- Tool completion rate.
- Returning users.
- Related-tool click rate.
- Revenue / 1,000 sessions after monetization.
- Hosting + service cost as % of revenue.
- Share of revenue concentrated in top 10 tools.
- % of live tools receiving meaningful usage.

## Strategic constraints
- No bought/low-quality traffic for the purpose of inflating ad activity.
- No misleading monetization UX.
- No fabricated platform statistics or guaranteed earnings claims.
- No expansion into unrelated niches merely to increase page count.
